Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner, fryer, and oven adds heat, the exhaust hoods pull conditioned air outside all shift long, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Garland HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep both the kitchen and the dining room comfortable, and we schedule visits around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical concerns for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. We provide detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits your operating schedule. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and guests comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Badly installed or neglected HVAC equipment can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and expensive water damage from clogged condensate lines. That's a real liability for any commercial property owner responsible for employees, customers, and tenants. Our commercial installations and inspections cover heat exchanger integrity, venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge, condensate drainage, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

Every hour without climate control costs a business money. For a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. In a retail store, it empties the aisles on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it means overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Garland HVAC provides fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Garland and surrounding areas, with an experienced technician dispatched as soon as you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on rooftop and split systems. The priority is pinpointing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your facility files.
