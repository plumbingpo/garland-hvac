Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in San Antonio

Most mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are easy. Swap your filter every one to three months, keep plants and shrubs at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and hose off the condenser coil every spring. Then book a professional tune-up twice a year, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

Age matters too. Most central air conditioners run 12 to 15 years, furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15 with regular service. If your system is past that window and showing symptoms, such as rising energy bills, rooms that never feel comfortable, or repairs stacking up season after season, a planned replacement is nearly always less expensive and far less stressful than an emergency one in the middle of a cold snap.

Minor issues don't stay minor for long. Consider a weak capacitor: replacing it is a short, affordable repair, but left alone for a season, it can take the compressor with it, and suddenly a minor fix has become a full system replacement.
